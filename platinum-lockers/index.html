The timeline

Six roles, one locker company

Each chapter asked for something different. The constant was making lockers easy to understand, rent and run.

  1. April 2019
    Product Marketing Manager

    Making sure everyone knew about the lockers

    Arenas, music festivals and more: the job was making sure every guest knew the lockers were there and understood how to rent one.

    • Upgraded the brand, logo and website
    • Built customer and investor presentations
    • Researched users and turned what they needed into personas
    LocketGo
  2. 2019
    A first break

    I stepped away

    In between, I spent time as Education Manager at Lighthouse Labs, supporting career-change cohorts.

  3. February 2020
    Product Marketing Manager, again

    Rehired, just in time for everything to change

    I came back to LocketGo to pick up where I'd left off on marketing the lockers.

    LocketGo
  4. Spring 2020
    COVID

    Events stopped, and I was let go

    With arenas and festivals shut down overnight, the business that depended on crowds paused.

  5. 3 months
    Pivot partner to the founder

    A COVID pivot to contactless delivery lockers

    I kept working with the founder to rethink the company around contactless delivery, and reworked the branding to match the new direction.

    • Repositioned the product for merchants and residences
    • New branding for the contactless offer
    LocketGo
  6. Several months
    Product Manager

    Building the contactless delivery software

    I turned customer needs into specifications and owned the backlog while the team built the new platform.

    • Customer interviews, mockups and functional specifications
    • Backlog prioritization in Jira, using Scrum
    • Test cases and user acceptance testing
    LocketGo
  7. Launch
    Operations and Product Manager

    Putting lockers on the street

    Once the software was ready, I moved into operations to deploy it: lockers on street corners where merchants drop off purchases for pickup, plus residential and parcel lockers.

    • Coordinated deployments and launches with internal and external partners
    • Product demos and a customer success workflow
    • Defined and tracked success metrics
    LocketGo
  8. Pause
    Moving on

    I left the company

  9. Acquisition
    Project Manager

    Back on board for the acquisition

    When Platinum Lockers acquired LocketGo, I came back to manage the different projects that came with the acquisition.

    Platinum Lockers
  10. Today
    Director of Operations

    Running operations across North America

    I coordinate operations across customer locations, technology vendors, payment partners and internal teams, and lead customer communications and transition planning during complex service changes.

    Platinum Lockers
