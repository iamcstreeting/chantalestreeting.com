What experience brings

The extras nobody asks for,
until they're missing

Québec's Law 25

Analytics stay off until a visitor says yes, and the box is unticked by default.

Policies with a paper trail

A privacy policy, cookie page and terms, each with a dated change log.

Security headers

Rules that control what the browser is allowed to load.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords and keys kept out of the code, and the code repository kept private.

The cost of data

Some sources charge per event, so how often you scan affects the budget.

Platform risk

Some sources don't want to be read by a program. That's worth knowing before you depend on them.

Found by search and AI

Guide pages written for search engines and AI assistants, which mostly skip the interactive map.

The finishing touches

A proper 404 page, share buttons and link previews.

Brand consistency

One name, spelled one way, everywhere it appears.
